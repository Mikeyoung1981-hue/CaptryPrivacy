Captry Privacy Policy

Photos & Camera

The camera is only on while you're on the capture screen. Photos are saved into Captry's own sandboxed storage on your device. Captry never writes to your iPhone Photos library, never shares photos with other apps, and never uploads them to a server.

Account Data

If you create an account, we store your email address so you can log in and reset your password. We may also store your username and anonymous account ID. We use Firebase Authentication and Firestore for sign-in and account data.

What We Send To Our Servers

Only account data such as your email, username, account ID, and the date you joined.

What We Don’t Do

We do not sell or rent your data. We do not use your photos to train AI. We do not track you across other apps or websites. We do not share data with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics companies.

Storage & Security

Photos live in Captry’s app sandbox on your iPhone. Other apps cannot read them. iOS encrypts that storage with your device passcode.

Children’s Privacy

Captry is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete your data from inside the app. We will permanently delete your account and server-side data within 30 days.

Changes To This Policy

If we change anything material, we will show a notice in the app before you continue.

Contact

Questions or requests? Email support@captry.ca
